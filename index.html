Understand what a website is asking you to do.

ClickTrap checks webpage text and interface characteristics for signals associated with common deceptive design patterns.

🧭

Misleading button labels

Highlight wording that may make an action unclear or steer users toward an unintended choice.

🍪

Confusing consent options

Review cookie prompts where accepting and rejecting options may be presented unequally.

⏳

Fake urgency & scarcity

Flag phrases and interface signals that create pressure through countdowns or limited-stock claims.

💳

Hidden costs

Look for fee disclosures and preselected paid add-ons that deserve a closer review.

🪤

Forced actions

Identify language that may push users to share details or take actions before proceeding.

↩️

Obstructive cancellation

Flag wording that suggests cancellation may involve extra steps or unnecessary barriers.

How ClickTrap works

A transparent, rule-based workflow that turns suspicious interface signals into understandable explanations.

01 / INSPECT

Choose a webpage

Use the browser extension on a webpage, try one of the included sample pages, or paste HTML into the dashboard.

02 / ANALYSE

Apply detection rules

JavaScript checks selected text, controls, and DOM characteristics against predefined patterns and heuristics.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Review the explanation

See the flagged evidence, why it was highlighted, and a practical action to consider before continuing.

Important: A ClickTrap warning is an informational signal, not proof that a website is fraudulent or legally non-compliant. Rule-based detection can produce false positives and may miss patterns.
